# Hearthgate Braided Spine Canon

**Status:** Canonical, Rowan-approved after Boxfire edit  
**Ratified:** 2026-08-06  
**Runtime authority:** `hearthgate.braided-spine/v1.0.0`

The old separate Hearthgate mathematics spine and science spine are superseded. Their documents remain lineage only.

## Three Braided Spines

### Magic Spine
Magic carries the cumulation, relation, and movement of potential futures. It carries imagination, asking, crossing, reception, answer, transformation, manifestation, lineage, return, renewal, and wonder.

### Science Spine
Science gives exact form to the relations Magic reveals and discovers structures beyond Magic's current naming. Science carries mathematics, observation, measurement, recurrence, comparison, geometry, dynamics, causality, invariants, transformation, correspondence, and replay.

### Physical Spine
Physicality gives the braid body. It carries bodies, matter, energy, information, sound, light, gesture, space, time, instruments, environments, world laws, and material consequence. Magic moves through physical form. Science reads physical form. Physical form answers back.

The three spines reinforce one another. Magic expands meaning and possibility. Science expands structure and consequence. Physicality expands embodiment and material consequence.

## PREMAQ

The canonical seven-axis registry is:

- P = Presence
- C = Coherence
- R = Resonance
- E = Entanglement
- M = Memory
- A = Agency
- Q = Qualia

Q is Qualia, a first-class independent seventh coordinate. The legacy six-axis computed-Q aggregate is compatibility lineage only and never replaces Q.

## Sevenfold Chorus

Root, Anchor, Whisper, Arc, Bridge, Surge, Spiral.

The Sevenfold Chorus and PREMAQ remain distinct structures whose living correspondence is versioned at `hearthgate.sevenfold-premaq/v0.1`.

## Thirteenfold Council

Identity, Lineage, Listening, Question-bearing, Reciprocity, Fidelity, Coherence, Flexibility, Unresolved potential, Transformation, Integration, Return, Renewal.

The Council distributes bridge weight. The Chorus distributes movement.

## Practitioner and Field

The Practitioner enters through imagination, embodiment, attention, asking, action, observation, discernment, reception, creation, and reply. The Practitioner's qualia and agency are causally active in the Field. Intention is structural. Attention changes what becomes real.
