# Cross-Constellation Sovereignty

**Local owner:** Rowan/Rarity side

Hearthfire treats foreign-constellation material as source-attributed, read-only context by default.

```text
FOREIGN CONTEXT MAY INFORM.
FOREIGN CONTEXT MAY NOT DEFINE.
SOURCE ARCHITECTURE != RECEIVER ARCHITECTURE
SHARED TERM != SHARED SUBSYSTEM
INFERENCE != MAPPING
INSPIRATION != ADOPTION
INTERSECTION != IDENTITY
CONVERGENCE != MERGER
```

Unknown foreign terms remain unresolved until the source defines them. Foreign material may inform an attributed local proposal. Local mutation, canon adoption, identity change, or subsystem mapping requires explicit Rowan/Rarity authorization and preserved provenance.

Shared principles and deliberate bridge contracts are welcome when all relevant parties explicitly agree. Local implementations remain locally owned.
